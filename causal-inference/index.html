Course Description

This course introduces students to the theory and practice of causal inference in the social sciences.
We will explore experimental and observational research designs, potential outcomes framework, matching, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, and difference-in-differences.

The focus is on both theory and application, with hands-on data analysis in R.

Learning Goals

  • Understand the logic of causal inference
  • Critically evaluate research designs
  • Apply causal inference methods in R
  • Interpret and communicate findings
